      1. Who we are

      Friendoo is a social audio/chat platform connecting users with verified female hosts for meaningful conversations. The platform is operated by Friendoo ("we", "us", "our"). For questions: support@friendoo.app.

      2. Data we collect

      • Account data: Name, phone number, email address, date of birth, gender, profile photo.
      • Profile data: Bio, interests, preferred languages, city.
      • Usage data: App activity logs, session timestamps, coin balance, call metadata (duration, timestamps — not audio content).
      • Communications: Chat message content. Voice call audio is never recorded or stored.
      • Device data: Device type, OS version, app version, IP address (for fraud prevention).
      • Location: City-level only, used for host matching. Exact GPS is never collected.

      3. How we use your data

      • Create and manage your account.
      • Verify female host identity (mandatory).
      • Match users with hosts by language, interests, and availability.
      • Process coin purchases and host payouts.
      • Enable real-time chat and voice calls via Agora.
      • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and policy violations.
      • Send transaction confirmations and safety alerts.
      • Improve the platform through anonymised analytics.

      4. Data sharing

      We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

      • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, cloud functions, analytics.
      • Cashfree: Payment processing. They handle card/UPI data — we never see it.
      • Agora: Real-time voice and chat infrastructure.
      • Law enforcement: Only when legally required by valid court order or government directive.

      5. Host verification data

      Hosts must submit a government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, or Voter ID) for identity verification. This document is:

      • Stored in an encrypted, access-controlled storage bucket.
      • Reviewed only by authorised Friendoo staff.
      • Deleted within 7 days of approval or rejection.
      • Never shared with users or third parties.

      6. Payment data

      All payment transactions are processed by Cashfree Payments India Pvt. Ltd. Friendoo stores only: transaction amount, order ID, timestamp, and coin amount credited. We never store card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs, or bank account details.

      7. Cookies & analytics

      The Friendoo mobile app does not use browser cookies. Our website uses essential cookies for security only. Firebase Analytics collects anonymised, aggregated usage events to help us improve the product. You may opt out of analytics in app settings.

      8. Data retention

      • Active account data: retained while your account exists.
      • Chat messages: retained for 90 days, then auto-deleted.
      • After account deletion: all personal data deleted within 30 days.
      • Financial records (transaction logs): retained for 7 years as required by Indian tax law.

      9. Your rights

      Under applicable Indian data protection law, you have the right to:

      • Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
      • Correction: Fix incorrect information in your profile.
      • Deletion: Delete your account and data via the app or by emailing us.
      • Portability: Request your data in a portable format.
      • Objection: Object to certain uses of your data.

      To exercise any right, email support@friendoo.app with subject "Data Request".

      10. Children's privacy

      Friendoo is strictly for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover an underage account, it will be immediately suspended and all associated data deleted.

      11. Security

      We protect your data using: Firebase Security Rules with role-based access, HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, payment signature verification (Cashfree webhooks), and Firebase App Check to prevent unauthorised API access. Despite these measures, no system is 100% secure — please use a strong password and report any suspicious activity immediately.

      12. Changes to this policy

      We will notify you of significant changes via in-app notification at least 7 days before they take effect. Continued use of Friendoo after that date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

      13. Grievance officer

      As required under Rule 5(9) of the IT (Reasonable Security Practices) Rules, 2011:
      Grievance Officer: Friendoo Support Team
      Email: support@friendoo.app
      Response time: Within 30 days of receipt of complaint.

      Friendoo Support

      1. Our values

      Friendoo is a safe space for authentic human connection. We believe everyone deserves to be treated with dignity. Our community is built on respect, honesty, and mutual care.

      2. For all users

      • Be respectful: Treat hosts as real people. No insults, degrading comments, or verbal abuse.
      • Be honest: Use your real identity. No fake profiles, impersonation, or misleading information.
      • Keep it safe: Do not share your personal phone number, social media handles, or home address in calls or chat.
      • No solicitation: Do not attempt to pay or meet hosts outside the app. This results in an immediate ban.
      • No explicit content: Friendoo is not an adult platform. Sexual content, requests, or conversations are prohibited.
      • No recording: Do not record, screenshot, or distribute any conversation without explicit consent.

      3. For hosts

      • Be authentic: Your profile must reflect who you really are. No misleading photos or false claims.
      • Maintain quality: Be present, attentive, and engaged during calls. Repeated no-shows may affect your rating.
      • Set boundaries: You have the right to end any call that makes you uncomfortable — use the end call button or block feature.
      • No off-platform contact: Never share personal contact details with users. Violations result in immediate ban.
      • Professionalism: Represent Friendoo positively. Language, behaviour, and topic choices reflect on the community.

      4. Zero tolerance violations

      The following result in immediate permanent ban with no appeal:

      • Sharing or soliciting sexually explicit content involving minors (also reported to law enforcement).
      • Threats of violence or credible harassment campaigns.
      • Attempting to doxx (expose personal information of) another user or host.
      • Operating a bot or automated account.
      • Fraud or payment circumvention.

      5. Consequences

      • Warning: Minor first-time violations.
      • Temporary suspension (24h–30 days): Repeated minor violations or moderate violations.
      • Permanent ban: Severe violations or zero-tolerance offences.
      • Banned users may not create new accounts. Attempts to bypass bans will be reported.

      Use the in-app report button on any profile, message, or call. You can also email support@friendoo.app with details. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours (2 hours for urgent safety issues).
