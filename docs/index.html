      1. Who we are

      Friendoo is an India-first social platform connecting users with verified female hosts. Contact: support@friendoo.app.

      2. What data we collect

      • Account: Name, phone/email, DOB, gender, profile photo.
      • Profile: Bio, interests, languages, city.
      • Usage: Activity logs, session timestamps, coin usage, call metadata.
      • Communication: Chat messages. Call audio is NOT recorded.
      • Payment: Transaction amounts, order IDs. Card/UPI details handled by Cashfree.
      • Host verification: ID documents stored securely, deleted after approval.

      3. How we use your data

      • Verify female host identity.
      • Match users with hosts by language and interests.
      • Process coin purchases.
      • Enable chat and voice calls.
      • Enforce safety and moderation.
      • Calculate and pay host earnings.

      4. Data sharing

      We do not sell your data. Shared with: Firebase, Cashfree, Agora, and legal authorities when required.

      5. Cookies

      The app does not use cookies. Firebase Analytics collects anonymised events only.

      6. Data retention

      Data retained while account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion.

      7. Your rights

      • Access, correct, delete your data.
      • Export your data via email request.
      • Delete account in-app anytime.

      8. Children's privacy

      Strictly 18+. No data collected from minors.

      9. Payment data

      Cashfree processes all payments. Friendoo never stores card numbers or UPI PINs.

      10. Security

      Firebase Security Rules, HTTPS, payment signature verification, and App Check protect the platform.

      11. Changes

      Significant changes notified in-app. Continued use = acceptance.
